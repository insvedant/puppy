About the Pomsky

A cross between a Pomeranian and a Siberian Husky, the Pomsky has striking looks, often with blue eyes. Pomskies are playful and energetic and need daily activity.

  • Adult weight: 15–30 lb
  • Lifespan: 12–15 years
  • Grooming: Brush 3–4 times a week; heavy seasonal shedding
  • Good for: Active families, Houses with yards
