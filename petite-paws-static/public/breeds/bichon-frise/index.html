About the Bichon Frise

Bichons are happy, sociable and gentle. Their curly white coat sheds very little, and they get along with children, other dogs and visitors.

  • Adult weight: 7–12 lb
  • Lifespan: 14–15 years
  • Grooming: Brush 2–3 times a week; trim every 4–6 weeks
  • Good for: Families, Allergy-friendly, First-time owners
