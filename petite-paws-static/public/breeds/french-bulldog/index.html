About the French Bulldog

Frenchies are relaxed, funny and affectionate. They need little exercise and are happy in small homes, but they don't tolerate heat well.

  • Adult weight: 16–28 lb
  • Lifespan: 10–12 years
  • Grooming: Weekly brushing; clean facial folds
  • Good for: Apartments, Families, Low exercise
