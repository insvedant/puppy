Feeding a Toy Puppy: Schedules That Work

Tiny puppies have tiny stomachs and fast metabolisms. They need small meals often, especially in the first months.

How often

  • 8–12 weeks: 4 meals a day
  • 3–6 months: 3 meals a day
  • 6 months and up: 2 meals a day

What to feed

Choose a small-breed puppy formula with a small kibble size. Keep the food your puppy came home with for at least two weeks, then switch gradually over 7–10 days if you want to change brands. Mix a little more of the new food in each day.

Preventing low blood sugar

Hypoglycemia is the most common emergency in toy breeds. Never skip a meal, keep a high-calorie supplement gel at home, and offer a snack after long play sessions or travel.

Treats and water

Treats should stay under 10% of daily calories. Fresh water should be available at all times. Use a shallow bowl that a small puppy can reach easily.

Foods to avoid

Chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol (found in sugar-free gum), macadamia nuts and cooked bones are all dangerous for dogs.
