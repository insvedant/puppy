How to Choose the Right Small Breed for Your Home

Small dogs share a size category, not a personality. A Chihuahua and a Maltipoo can weigh the same and still need very different homes. Before you fall in love with a photo, check how each breed fits these four areas of your life.

1. Your space and schedule

Apartment living suits most toy breeds, but some handle being alone better than others. Maltese and Bichons are happiest with company for most of the day. Yorkies and Chihuahuas are a little more independent once they are adults. If you work long hours away from home, plan for a dog walker or daycare whichever breed you choose.

2. Allergies and shedding

If anyone in the home has allergies, look at low-shedding coats: Toy Poodle, Maltipoo, Maltese and Bichon Frise. No dog is fully hypoallergenic, so spend time around the breed before deciding.

3. Grooming time

A Pomeranian's double coat needs brushing three to four times a week. Poodle-type coats need a professional trim every six to eight weeks. A French Bulldog needs little brushing but regular cleaning of its facial folds. Grooming is a real cost in both time and money, so budget for it.

4. Children and other pets

Very small puppies can be hurt by an accidental drop or a rough game. With young children, a sturdier small breed such as a Maltipoo, Bichon or French Bulldog is often the safer choice.

Quick match guide

  • First-time owner: Maltipoo, Bichon Frise
  • Allergy-friendly: Toy Poodle, Maltese
  • Big personality: Pomeranian, Chihuahua
  • Low exercise: French Bulldog
  • Active family: Pomsky
