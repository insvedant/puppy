How do I reserve a puppy?

Message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form with the puppy's name. We confirm availability, set up a video call, and you secure your puppy with a deposit. The balance is due before delivery.

Can I see the puppy before I pay?

Yes. Every reservation starts with a live video call with the puppy so you can see its movement, coat and personality.

What comes with my puppy?

Vaccination record, deworming record, vet health certificate, microchip details, a written health guarantee and a starter kit with food, a blanket and a toy.

Do you deliver?

Yes. You can pick up in person, or we arrange delivery by pet cargo or a flight nanny who hands the puppy to you at your airport.

Is the deposit refundable?

Deposits are non-refundable but can be moved to another puppy if your chosen puppy does not pass its final vet check.

Message us and we'll set up a live video call today.
