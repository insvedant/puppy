About Petite Paws

A small, family-run breeding program focused on healthy, well-socialized toy breeds.

Our story

We started with one Pomeranian and a lot of questions about how to raise small dogs well. Today we work with a handful of carefully selected breeds and a dedicated vet partner.

Our puppies grow up inside our home, around everyday sounds and people. By the time they leave, they're used to handling, grooming and short car rides.

Our promise

  • Honest descriptions and real videos of every puppy
  • Written contract and health guarantee
  • Support for the life of your dog
