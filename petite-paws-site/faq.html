Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

How do I reserve a puppy?

Message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form with the puppy's name. We confirm availability, set up a video call, and you secure your puppy with a deposit. The balance is due before delivery.

Can I see the puppy before I pay?

Yes. Every reservation starts with a live video call with the puppy so you can see its movement, coat and personality.

What comes with my puppy?

Vaccination record, deworming record, vet health certificate, microchip details, a written health guarantee and a starter kit with food, a blanket and a toy.

Do you deliver?

Yes. You can pick up in person, or we arrange delivery by pet cargo or a flight nanny who hands the puppy to you at your airport.

Is the deposit refundable?

Deposits are non-refundable but can be moved to another puppy if your chosen puppy does not pass its final vet check.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Monthly payment plans are available through our financing partner, subject to approval.

How big will my puppy get?

Each listing shows an estimated adult weight based on the parents. Small breeds usually reach full size by 10–12 months.

What if my puppy has a health issue?

Our one-year health guarantee covers genetic and congenital conditions diagnosed by a licensed vet. See the Health Guarantee page for details.

Can I visit in person?

Yes, by appointment. Message us on WhatsApp to book a visit time.

How old are puppies when they go home?

Puppies go home at 10 weeks or older, once they are eating well, vaccinated and cleared by our vet.

Still have a question?

Message us on WhatsApp. We usually reply within minutes.
